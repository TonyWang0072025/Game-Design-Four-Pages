# Quality checks

## Vision
Can a newcomer explain what game is being made? Is the vision a concise direction rather than a feature inventory or marketing claim? Do not invent positioning facts.

## Pillars
Can each principle affect a real trade-off? Explain the phrase rather than relying on a catchy slogan. Separate candidate interpretations from confirmed decisions. Systems such as combat or crafting are not sufficient principles by themselves.

## Loops
Use readable action labels. Explain the cause or sequence behind each connection. Identify speculative or conditional paths. Show relevant longer loops without replacing the diagram with a complete UI map.

## Resource flow
Verify nouns and conversion verbs. Distinguish stock, state, information, results, occupancy, and loss. Do not imply a guaranteed return for every expense. Mark unimplemented relationships. Check potential feedback and bottlenecks without declaring a balance result from topology alone.

## Cross-document review
- Record current project sources and preserve decision status.
- Keep detailed specifications and change history elsewhere.
- Use explicit text labels as well as dashed edges or colors.
- Check relative links, Markdown fences, and diagram syntax; inspect rendered output when available. Disclose checks not performed.
- Test an incomplete project for invention and a concrete project for useful trade-off analysis.
- Do not redistribute full third-party slides, captions, or footage as repository-owned templates.
